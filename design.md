# Daivik Borewells

The existing single-page Next.js architecture remains the foundation. The redesign uses an open photographic hero, interactive property selector, horizontal equipment showcase, compact service rows, process timeline, property applications, service areas, FAQ and contact form.

## Visual System

Soft mint, powder blue, pale peach and lilac sit on a near-white ground. Dark green ink provides contrast. Instrument Serif is reserved for editorial headings; locally served DM Sans handles body copy and controls. All letter spacing is zero. Tokens live in tokens.css. Controls use 4px corners; sections stay unframed.

## Interaction

Contact actions remain visible. Property selection updates recommendations and carries the requirement into the site-visit form. The form validates four fields and opens a prepared WhatsApp message; nothing is submitted automatically or stored on the site. Native FAQ disclosures work without JavaScript. Spatial motion is disabled with reduced motion.

## Content Integrity

Use the supplied business name, phone and domain. Do not publish unverified years, project counts, water-detection percentages, client brands or customer reviews. Generated images illustrate equipment and property scenarios and are not records of completed projects. Gallery captions and the footer disclose this. Real portfolio and testimonial sections can be enabled when verified content is provided.
